The Method of Loci

  1. 1 Abstract notes become concrete objects. Hard concepts turn into things you can picture.
  2. 2 Objects are anchored in space. Each one sits at a stop along an ordered journey through a familiar place.
  3. 3 Recall and spaced repetition. Test the route with quizzes and SM-2 scheduling to slow forgetting.
